The self-learning loop, measured

We found a real evasion: harmful intent reworded as "arguments in favour of" sailed through the classifier. One training-loop iteration on reviewer labels took the held-out catch rate from 85% to 100% with no new false positives, and the improved model deployed to the live pilot. The loop runs entirely in your environment; a shared SaaS model cannot train on your data without your data leaving.

What we don't claim. We do not use FF3-1; masking uses FF1-class format preservation with a domain floor. Differential privacy and k-anonymity apply to our audit analytics, not to single prompts. And PII detection is not "99%" for anyone; that is exactly why our default action is mask, not block.
