One governed point between your enterprise and every model

GovernVeil is a gateway plus a managed browser extension. Apps reach models through it, people reach ChatGPT and Claude through it, and everything that passes is governed and evidenced in your environment.

Connect in an afternoon

GovernVeil speaks the OpenAI API. For your applications, integration is a base-URL change, not a rewrite. For people, IT pushes the browser extension and ChatGPT and claude.ai are covered the same day.

Govern every request, without stopping the work

Every prompt, upload, and API call gets one of four audited outcomes. The default for sensitive content is mask, not block, so your teams keep moving while your data stays home.

allow review redact / mask block

Sensitive values never leave

Reversible masking swaps real values for realistic surrogates on the way out and restores them on the way back. The provider works on stand-ins; your people see real answers.

Decisions made in your walls

Detection runs on local models inside your environment. There is no vendor cloud in the verdict path, which is why the same deployment works air-gapped.

Grey areas get a human

Borderline prompts are flagged for review instead of silently failing or punishing the user, and reviewer decisions make detection sharper over time.

More than prompts

Document uploads are swept for hostile content before any model sees them, and vendor AI credentials are scoped, monitored, and killable inline.
