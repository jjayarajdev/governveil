About GovernVeil

GovernVeil is the AI-governance platform from Syntegreti, the AI consulting and platform company in Hyderabad, India. We build AI data-egress control and evidence for regulated enterprises: BFSI, healthcare and life sciences, global capability centres, and any organisation whose data cannot leave its perimeter for a verdict.

How we work

Evidence first. Every claim on this site has a number, a mechanism, or a re-runnable harness behind it. Our detection benchmark is public and anyone can re-run it.

Self-hosted by conviction. The product runs where your data lives. There is no vendor cloud in the verdict path, which is why it works air-gapped and why it can train on your data without your data moving.

Honest about the roadmap. Features are labelled shipped or roadmap, on the site and in the sales conversation. Multi-tenancy and Splunk and Sentinel emitters are roadmap today; everything else you have read here is running.

The company behind it

Syntegreti ships flagship platforms for contracts and business operations, and underneath them one spine that governs how an enterprise adopts AI. GovernVeil is the govern layer of that spine: it intercepts every LLM call, enforces policy with local ML detection, and keeps a full audit log of all AI traffic. Delivery follows the same Catalyst discipline as the rest of the portfolio: a working result in your environment in weeks, not quarters.
