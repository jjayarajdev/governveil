Q · WHAT HAPPENS WHEN SOMETHING SENSITIVE APPEARS

Not allow-or-block. Keep the work moving, safely.

Every request reaches exactly one of five outcomes, and the reason is written down before the reply is sent.

ALLOW

Nothing sensitive. It goes through, and the decision is still recorded.

FLAG

Borderline. It proceeds and goes to a review queue rather than stopping the person.

MASK

The identifier is replaced before it leaves. The work still happens.

ACKNOWLEDGE

The person is shown what they are about to send, and says why.

BLOCK

It does not leave. The denial itself is the evidence.

Five outcomes · exactly one per request · each signed to the ledger

The differentiator against DLP tools that only allow or block. Mask and acknowledge are what let a bank say yes to AI instead of no. Do not describe reversible masking as on by default — it is opt-in.
